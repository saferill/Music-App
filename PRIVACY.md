# Privacy Policy for Sonara Music

**Effective Date:** September 2026

Sonara Music is an open-source, client-side application designed with user privacy as a foundational principle.

## 1. Information Collection
- **No Personal Data Collection:** Sonara Music does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, identifiers, or location data.
- **Local Storage:** All preferences, cached audio, playlists, favorites, and history are stored exclusively on your local device.

## 2. Third-Party Services
- Sonara Music interacts directly with public YouTube Music endpoints to stream audio and retrieve track metadata without requiring an account.
- If you optionally log in with a YouTube account, session cookies are stored strictly on your local device.

## 3. Telemetry & Analytics
- Sonara Music does not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or data broker integrations.

## 4. Open Source
The complete source code of Sonara Music is open source and available for review at:
https://github.com/saferill/Music-App

## 5. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via GitHub Issues:
